# 00 — Project Charter

## Identity
- Product: Lunéa (`lunea`, 루네아)
- Category: AI-assisted personal emotional journal
- Initial platform: mobile, iOS and Android target
- Initial language: Korean-first (proposed)
- Product owner: human
- Primary developer: Claude Code
- Independent reviewer: CodeRabbit (Codex deferred, D-21)
- Status: planning baseline; technical and visual decisions remain open

## Vision
Help people notice, name, preserve, and revisit the complex emotional texture of everyday life without judging it.

## Product promise
Users describe events in their own words. Lunéa helps explore possible emotions and context. Users decide what feels true and what becomes part of their journal.

## Non-negotiable principles
1. Human interpretation wins; AI proposes, user decides.
2. Multiple and conflicting emotions may coexist.
3. Preserve original wording; never overwrite it with AI summaries or taxonomy labels.
4. No moral score, positivity ranking, or judgment of a person/day.
5. No streak guilt or mandatory daily completion.
6. Journal and emotion data are sensitive personal data.
7. Quiet, accessible interaction; respect reduced motion.
8. AI may abstain or say it is unsure.
9. Work in small, verifiable tickets.
10. Human approval is required for privacy, security, data, AI-policy, and release decisions.

## MVP outcome
A user can sign up, understand AI/privacy boundaries, write a journal entry, optionally answer one clarification question, inspect event/emotion suggestions, accept/edit/reject/add labels, explicitly save, revisit/edit/delete records, and access data controls.

## Out of scope
Clinical diagnosis/treatment; social feed or public sharing; voice/image/video analysis; AI-initiated push; personality profiling; broad predictive analytics; payments before value validation; definitive Balance formula; final receipt/collection visual metaphor.

## Success milestone
A reliable end-to-end prototype: journal → AI suggestions → user correction → explicit save → retrieval, without losing original words or user control.
